Verzuz Setlist: Harmonic Analysis

BeBe & CeCe: "Close To You"
Arrangement Notes: Classic 90s R&B crossover ballad architecture. The keyboardist is leaning heavily into lush Rhodes voicing. Listen for the smooth voice-leading during the turnaround. They sequence through secondary dominants heavily extended with 11ths and 13ths to maintain a modern soul suspension rather than resolving strictly to standard triads.
Mary Mary: "Ordinary People"
Arrangement Notes: Warryn Campbell's signature production style. The harmonic movement here is less about sweeping chordal changes and more about the tight, aggressive minor-key vamp. The keys are playing rhythmic stabs locking in with the bass on m11 voicings, leaving massive space for the stacked vocal harmonies to dictate the chord qualities.
BeBe & CeCe: "I.O.U. Me"
Arrangement Notes: A masterclass in gospel/pop modulation. Pay attention to the bridge into the final chorus. The band uses a series of sus4 chords resolving to maj9 to lift the progression. The keyboard arrangement utilizes a very distinct 80s/90s layered synth-brass sound, voicing the chords with wide intervals (dropping the 3rd to the bass/tenor register) to make the track sound massive without muddying the vocals.
Mary Mary: "Shackles (Praise You)"
Arrangement Notes: This is a completely rhythmic-driven harmonic structure. The defining feature is the minor pentatonic groove. When the band swells, the keyboardist is hitting dominant 7#9 and 7b9 passing chords—very typical of contemporary black gospel—to create tension right before resolving back to the root minor vamp. The voice-leading in the turnaround is completely dictated by the syncopated kick drum pattern.
